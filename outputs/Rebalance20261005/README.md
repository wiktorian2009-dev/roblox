# Rebalance + 45 new plow parts (2026-10-05, Claude, Studio 4d5c9178)

Brief: BRIEF.md. Before/after tables: BEFORE_AFTER.md. Asset manifest: manifest.json. Gallery: gallery/.
Nothing was published. All numbers below are simulation estimates unless they are marked **real**.

## What was wrong before (diagnosis, 40 seeds)
- Progression was driven almost entirely by engines. Blades and gears gave only +3–8% per step, so most offers were "useless".
- Jobs paid 0.64× ordinary collecting, so taking a job was a loss.
- Long dead zones with nothing to buy:
  - Crystal → Retro: longest gap 19.5 / 28.5 min (median / P90) for the value buyer.
  - Absolute Zero for the legacy buyer: 36 / 57 min.
  - Longest unavailable stretch P90: 8.2 min (efficient) and 24.8 min (casual).
- Pity could hand out parts that took hours to afford.

## What changed
- **Catalog: 90 → 135 parts** (45 blades, 45 engines, 45 gears). Every old id is kept.
  - The new role **M** ("mid-band step") sits between a world's practical A part and its premium B part. Its price and power are interpolated geometrically between them; offer weight is 0.7.
  - New parts per slot are placed at the measured gaps: Cursed 1, Crystal 2, Retro 1, Jungle 1, AZ 2, Sakura 1, Candy 2, Atlantis 1, Toybox 1, Clockwork 2, Cloud 1.
  - Blades and gears now grow 1.35× / 1.18× per band, and engines are compensated so the total power per band is unchanged.
  - Premium steps: blade 1.45×, gear 1.3×, power 1.6×.
  - Ladder fixes: the Balanced Engine (power 3.5) is no longer stronger than Desert premium, and the legendary Zero-G gear (×4.0) now beats Glacier Tracks (×3.83).
  - The PlowConfig parts table is generated by `sim/gen_lua2.py` from `data/catalog_spec2.py` + `sim/catalog2.py` + `sim/final_variant.json`. Regenerate there rather than hand-editing prices.
- **Odds:** "practical" now means A or M parts of the current or previous band, still at least 45% of the pool. The odds shown on the card are exactly `C.OfferPool(band, luck).P`, which the server samples.
- **Pity** (after 6 non-useful rolls): a useful part from band ≤ current + 1 that the player can afford with Snow in hand plus 10 minutes of recent income (`Shop.ProtectAffordMinutes`). If none is affordable, it falls back to any useful part.
- **Prices never rise silently:** `C.OfferPrice(o)` returns the stored offer or reservation price, or the current catalog price if that is lower. Both the shown price and the Buy price use it, so price drops are passed on.
- **Stage gates:**

| Stage | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Retro | 260B | 180B |
| Jungle | 1.6T | 2.2T |
| Absolute Zero | 17T | 22T |
| Portal | 600T | 700T |
| Candy | 37Qa | 60Qa |
| Atlantis | 230Qa | 370Qa |
| Toybox | 670Qa | 1.1Qi |
| Clockwork | 3.1Qi | 5Qi |
| Cloud | 29Qi | 46Qi |
| Cosmic | 260Qi | 420Qi |

  - Desert, Cursed and Crystal are unchanged.
  - Stage eggs from Retro onward and all World 2 `W2_*` upgrades are scaled by the same ratio as their stage (Sakura uses the Portal ratio).
  - World 1 upgrade prices follow StageConfig automatically.
- **Jobs** (ContractConfig.Reward + ContractService):
  - Reward = rate × (2.0 × expected cycle − 0.8 × expected clearing time). The cycle is clearing time from zone area / (blade swath × lane speed) + 35 s travel + 20 s overhead.
  - Including the Snow from job tiles, a job pays **2.0×** ordinary collecting over the full cycle, and **2.5×** for demanding jobs (Market, Pump, Workshop).
  - Temporary Snow boosts are divided out when the reward is snapshotted, and the boost active at completion is applied once at payout (new in this pass). Job tiles pay through the normal boosted payout, so nothing is double-counted.
- **Achievements:** ids are unchanged. Parts90 now reads "Own 90 different plow parts", and **Parts135** "Grand Garage" (Own all 135, +6% luck) is new.
- **Art:** 45 Blender models in 3 packs: Blades_C 82296300053310, Engines_C 98030263111271, Gears_C 114784222969268.
  - Sources: outputs/PlowExpansion20261005/parts/blades2.py, engines2.py, gears2.py, build_new.py, PlowParts45new.blend, fbx/*_C.fbx and upload_new.py.
  - Studio: the models are in ReplicatedStorage.PlowParts (attribute Rebalance=2026-10-05); the raw packs are in ServerStorage.PlowExpansionImports.Rebalance.

## Studio tests (Play with BalanceTestMode, attribute removed afterwards; temporary runtime helper scripts existed only during Play and are gone)

**Real (server handlers, live data, real GUI input):**
- Catalog and models:
  - 135 parts (45/45/45), every model present; all 90 old ids kept.
  - 91,125 blade × engine × gear combinations give finite stats; spawn falls back to the starter part for an unknown id (PlowService line 212).
- Buying:
  - Buy fails at 299 for a 300-Snow part and succeeds at 300 (Snow 0, part owned).
  - Replaying the same token and a forged token are both rejected.
  - Migration: a stored price of 99,999 is shown and charged at the 1,500 catalog price. A reservation stored at 1,000 (catalog 900K) keeps 1,000, fails at 999 Snow and succeeds at 1,000.
- Equipping:
  - Equip, wrong-slot and not-owned checks behave as expected; unequip puts the starter part back.
- New parts:
  - All 45 were offered, bought and equipped through the real handler, and all 45 assemble (PlowAssembly.Build + PartModel).
- Pity (band 7, 10T budget): 9/9 protected rolls were useful and affordable (Cryo Compressor 6.6T). With the budget too small it falls back to useful parts (15T–1.7Qa). The dry counter resets.
- Odds:
  - The shown roll chance equals the server pool (Sand Runner 6.2% at band 1 with 2.85 luck during the Lucky Wind event).
  - 52 real rolls at band 7 match pool group shares within sampling noise:

| Group | Expected | Observed |
|---|---|---|
| Practical | 0.54 | 0.63 |
| Premium now | 0.13 | 0.13 |
| Older | 0.10 | 0.10 |
| Newer | 0.23 | 0.13 |

- Jobs:
  - Offers for all 8 types at Starter pay reward/rate 152 s, and 200 s for demanding jobs. At Jungle and AZ, Road 151–154 s and Pump/Workshop 193–198 s, i.e. 2.0× / 2.5× of the full cycle with tile Snow included.
  - Completed job with a 2× temporary boost: reward 1,600 → paid 3,200. Without a boost: reward = paid.
- Rebirth through the real RequestRebirth remote: Snow 0 and stages reset; 6 parts and the build kept.
- GUI:
  - Index shows "PARTS x/135" with 45 cards per tab and updates to 90/135 after granting.
  - The workbench lists 90 owned rows, including the new parts, with a working 3D preview. A real click equipped the new Bonechill Scoop.
  - The real BUY button bought Dune Sweeper.
- Driving:
  - Early: starter build on the Starter field earned **328 Snow in ~17 s** (model 24/s ≈ real 19/s).
  - Mid: Jungle field with Jungle M parts and simulated upgrade multipliers earned 7.9e8 in 16 s. A cleaner forward-only sample was distorted by batched payouts and by the game resetting the test multipliers, so **mid-game income is not calibrated by these short runs**.
  - Late: not driven. The late band was covered by real purchase and equip tests of all 45 parts.
- No server errors from the game scripts; the only console errors came from my own test commands.
